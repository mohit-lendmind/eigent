# Head of Sales perspective on mesh-m4-connectors

## What I support
M4 is my strongest demo: the agent driving **MSE live in the adviser's own logged-in browser** (LB4, visible window, take-control) → full result set incl. declines, why-not per rejected lender, coverage statement → immutable hash-chained snapshot in the audit record. The wow: "watch it operate a tool you already use, and every step lands in the compliance file." I sell the **audit output**, not the automation.

## What I want changed (Dissent:)
- **Dissent: "MSE-live + a framework for your Mortgage Brain" closes a design *partner*, not a paid *reference*.** §11 partners co-build free — scaffold + authored replay eval ("goes green the day you record a session") is enough for them. Do NOT pitch a paid reference firm on `verified:false` scaffolds; it needs its own adapter verified first.
- **Dissent: never demo the licensed scaffold as if it runs** — show the framework + the red eval honestly, or a rival brands it vaporware, losing our compliance-trust moat.

## What I would not ship without
- A **named design partner + which licensed tool is theirs** (open question 1), or we guess which scaffold to verify first.
- The **recorded-session ask as a 10-min trial-day-1 activity**, not procurement: "screen-share one Mortgage Brain search, we replay it in CI." Their login, LB4 — nothing egresses. That de-risks the handover and breaks the chicken-and-egg: MSE-live is the value proof that earns the session.
- **Enterprise:** audit + retention + data-residency I sell today; SSO/RBAC deferred to a named milestone, never silently absent.

## Acceptance criteria from my lens
- 5-min cold demo: agent sources MSE live, adviser sees the shortlist (adviser-only; G5 = human pick), snapshot + coverage statement visible in the export.
- "Add a login → eval goes green" is a runnable, documented demo step.
- Written security answer: connector runs under the adviser's own login — no credential handling by us, no third-party processor.

## Edge cases I want addressed
- "Is this whole-of-market?" — coverage statement on-screen, not buried in config.
- "What if the portal changes?" — show nightly replay catching drift; reliability sells the compliance buyer.

## What I must NOT claim
- **Whole-of-market via MSE** — it's Podium best-buys (direct + most broker deals), never whole-of-market (invariant 4 / MCOB 4.4A.4R(3)).
- **A live or verified licensed adapter** — all `verified:false` until replay-green against a real recorded session.
- **"Evidence of research" completeness off MSE alone** — MSE-only is not a whole-panel research record.
